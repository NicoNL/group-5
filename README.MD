🐘 What’s Jungle Juggle About?

Welcome to the wild world of Jungle Juggle, a fun and challenging safari management game set in the heart of Africa! You're in charge of building and running your own safari park — think of it as managing a zoo without fences, and with a lot more chaos.

Your job? Keep your animals happy, your tourists excited, and your wallet full. You'll be balancing nature, business, and the occasional poacher while growing your park into a thriving attraction.
🕹️ Gameplay Highlights

    Top-Down Safari Map: You manage your park from a bird's-eye view. The map is bigger than what you see — you can scroll around or use the minimap.

    Time Control: Play at your own pace by speeding up or slowing down time (hour/day/week).

    Difficulty Levels: Choose your challenge — easy, medium, or hard, each with its own win conditions.

🌿 Environment

    The map includes bushes, trees, grass, and small water sources. You can add more greenery or even build ponds to help your animals thrive.

🦁 Animals

    Herbivores munch on plants and chill in groups.

    Carnivores prefer to snack on herbivores (sorry, nature).

    Animals drink water, age over time, and may reproduce if well-fed and in the right group.

    They wander the map and rest when full, but if hungry or thirsty, they’ll seek out known food or water spots.

🚙 Jeeps and Roads

    Tourists come in jeeps you buy. Each jeep carries up to 4 visitors.

    Build roads connecting your park's entrance and exit — jeeps need a path to follow!

    More animals = happier tourists = more money!

💰 Money & Winning

Start with a bit of capital. You’ll spend it on:

    Animals

    Plants

    Roads

    Jeeps

    Rangers, and more...

Earn money through tourism and selling animals (ethically, we hope).
Win the Game:

Keep enough animals, tourists, and profit for a certain number of months — depends on your chosen difficulty.
Lose the Game:

    Go bankrupt

    Or... all your animals die (yeah, that’s game over)
